## Reviewer-approved architecture

Additive CAP_GPU_BUFFER_HANDLES, dynamic guest/wire tables; old modules retain existing limits until
explicit negotiation. Other GPU namespaces and GPU byte budgets unchanged. Both native backends and
validator/recorder need12,000+ CC0 coverage. Preserve old wire streams below pressure.
One BGT buffer per guest store avoids suballocation aliasing/migration and preserves all map/orphan,
transform-feedback, uniform-block and alignment semantics. No finite shared uniform-count pool may
starve vertices. Final gates and acceptance remain pending.
