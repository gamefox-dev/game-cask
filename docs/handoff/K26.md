## Q73 cause and approved design (#227)

K21 times individual sampled interpreter dispatches and extrapolates exclusive sample microseconds
by category calls/samples (~1024). Sampling pays clock/instrumentation overhead that unsampled dispatches
do not pay; scaling that overhead does not estimate a physical exclusive budget. Millions of samples
cannot cure this systematic bias. Existing coverage-convergence docs explicitly permit >wall; that
contract must be corrected. Legacy category/API timers remain inclusive diagnostics, not partitions.

The actual timer-only CC0 regression (100,000 dispatches, a deterministic clock increment per import)
produces an extrapolation >100 times its entire elapsed clock interval without any guest work.
This proves a sufficient accounting error, not a quantitative decomposition of the porter's trace.
No evidence of a clock-source or Asyncify double-count root cause has been established here.

Reviewer #227 approved **coarse non-extrapolated disjoint exclusive spans**, clocks only at
fallback/native/thunk/scheduler transitions and flush across yield/return. Approved small scope beyond
bgt/runtime: x86_rt.c, x86_profile.h, w32_core.c serializer/idle hooks. Preserve raw sampled diagnostics;
summary uses physical intervals, must be <= update wall, match hand-timed fixture, and measure clock
overhead itself. I13 is merging into related files: keep diff small. Build/emitter fixes still require
another reviewer decision after measured attribution.
