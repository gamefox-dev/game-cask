## Remaining operational limits

No open requested feature/blocker. Bridge state is same-user loopback coordination, not authentication or
power-loss/multiwriter storage. PID probes denied by the sandbox fall back to heartbeat expiry; legacy records
without PID evidence need operator release. Release/lease expiry revokes ownership but does **not** kill an old
worker or its descendants. Stop/verify old workers before operator release.

Context tokens are host estimates, not billing measurements; checkpoint steering asks the lane to commit/handoff/stop
rather than forcibly terminating it. Memory gating is admission scheduling, not a reservation or guarantee against
later pressure; direct node/zig/browser commands are not intercepted. Use the documented workspace npm/npx gate.
Keep private agent/session/provider artifacts in scratch; do not copy them into Git.
